# Color Hierarchy

## Principle

Color can attract attention, group information, signal state, and establish brand character. Its strongest use should correspond to meaningful priority.

## Trigger

Activate when many accent colors compete, when every button is visually loud, or when a brand palette needs to coexist with semantic status colors.

## Non-designer question

> 页面里真正需要被“点亮”的地方有几个？如果所有地方都很醒目，用户还知道哪里最重要吗？

## Decision

Keep the accent budget intentional. Use neutral surfaces and text roles to carry most of the interface, reserving stronger chroma or contrast for meaningful emphasis.

## Specification implications

Define:

- dominant neutral roles
- accent role
- action role
- status roles
- emphasis tiers

## Verification

Temporarily remove color and compare hierarchy. Then restore color and check that accents reinforce rather than create a contradictory hierarchy.

## Common failures

- rainbow dashboards
- every CTA using the strongest accent
- status colors confused with brand accents
- using gradients as a substitute for hierarchy
