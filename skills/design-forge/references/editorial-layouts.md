# Editorial Layouts

Use when a design needs strong reading hierarchy, asymmetric composition, pacing, or content-led visual rhythm.

## What to inspect

- editorial hierarchy rather than card grids
- asymmetric alignment
- headline/body scale contrast
- whitespace as pacing
- image-to-text relationships
- section rhythm across long pages

## Why it matters

Editorial systems are useful when the user's content is the product. They can create a strong identity without relying on decorative effects.

## Design question

> How can the page feel composed like a publication while remaining efficient to scan on the web?

## Use with

- cinematic experiences
- personal archives
- long-form knowledge bases
- cultural/editorial sites

Do not copy a publication's surface styling. Extract hierarchy, pacing, and composition principles.
