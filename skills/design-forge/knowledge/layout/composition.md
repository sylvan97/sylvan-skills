# Composition

## Principle

Composition controls how elements occupy space and how the eye moves through a page. A successful composition creates intentional relationships between subject, support, and empty space.

## Trigger

Activate for editorial, cinematic, marketing, portfolio, landing, or image-led experiences; also when a layout feels flat despite correct components.

## Non-designer question

> 你希望用户的视线像“阅读一篇杂志”一样被引导，还是像“使用一个工具”一样快速找到目标？

## Decision

Choose a dominant compositional structure before decorating: focal point, reading path, alignment system, and negative-space strategy.

## Specification implications

- Define focal regions.
- Define alignment anchors and container behavior.
- Define image/text relationships.
- Define intentional asymmetry or symmetry.
- Define how composition transforms at smaller viewports.

## Verification

Use a two-second glance test and a grayscale check. The intended focal point should remain legible without relying on color alone.

## Common failures

- decorative symmetry without hierarchy
- random asymmetry
- hero sections that consume space without communicating priority
- treating desktop composition as a collection of independent cards
