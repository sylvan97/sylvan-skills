# Readability

## Principle

Readable text depends on typography, language, contrast, line length, spacing, content structure, and viewing context together.

## Trigger

Activate for article pages, documentation, long descriptions, knowledge bases, mobile reading, and multilingual content.

## Non-designer question

> 用户是在“扫信息”，还是会真正坐下来读这段内容？两种场景需要的文字节奏一样吗？

## Decision

Optimize text for its reading task. Use appropriate measure, line-height, paragraph spacing, contrast, and responsive behavior.

## Specification implications

Define:

- readable measure / max line width
- body line-height
- paragraph rhythm
- heading-to-body relationships
- link and inline emphasis behavior
- mobile reading adjustments

## Verification

Read a representative long-form section at the intended viewport and zoom. Check scanning, sustained reading, and link recognition.

## Common failures

- desktop-length lines on large screens
- tiny body text used to fit more content
- weak paragraph rhythm
- low-contrast secondary text
- treating mobile as a scaled desktop reading layout
